# Design briefs

A milestone's design starts from briefs: each reads a group of research reports in full and
digests what they say for TCE, with citations to report sections (`04-09 §5.3` is section 5.3 of
`research/04-people/09-criminology.md`). The design then takes its decisions in the ADRs
(`decisions/`) and the plan's decision log (`PROJECT_PLAN.md` §9).

A brief is an input, not a decision. Where a brief and an ADR or the plan differ, the ADR or the
plan wins. Check a brief's citations against the reports before relying on one.
