## What this branch implements

fire caution survives load; interrupted deliveries retain their accounting; river overrides
hold their requested days and route only added flow downstream; storm direction is used;
covering groups are evaluated once per storm pulse; flood store losses are charged once per
day and supplies arrive at the household's chosen refuge. The journal records larger BF
accounting/calibration concerns instead of pretending its dashboard row is graded.

BD adds no-water stripping/salvage choices, monthly household-fire summaries, crew demolition
with tools/eligibility/ownership or current leave, dated found-burning accounts passed through
ordinary contact, chronological fire-page reasons and the Help at a fire lens. Physical task
awareness follows personally known connected fire scenes. The ignition sensitivity runner and
CSV/report live in `docs/calibration/fire-ignition-sweep.*`. The Fires row separates observer
episode losses. The small sweep is not a complete response calibration; default spontaneous
demolition crews have not been demonstrated. Large-population lens performance is unproved.

BG is **partially implemented**, not done:

- Saved sub-grid straight bank segments, trapezoidal fill and dedicated borrow pits. Initial
  construction consumes source earth at the saved compaction ratio. No production agent
  creates a bank yet; `bank_fixture` is a controlled engineering/snapshot example.
- Conservative connected river-flood storage includes bank-associated changed ground and
  borrow depressions, even after breach. Crests retain overtopping and open-end passage.
  Unaffected reaches retain BF's calculation; ordinary legacy earthworks do not silently
  change drainage. Reserved envelopes must contain the complete trapezoid and fit the map.
- Actual daily overtopping lowers the crest. Removed earth stays in a saved washed-sediment
  stock attached to its bank; ground/replay use remaining fill. The stock does not imply a
  river destination or hydraulic obstruction. This is a coarse crest/volume model, not a
  detailed eroded breach cross-section. Wear uses the elapsed day's depths before weather
  rollover, so a one-day forcing counts; repeated depth queries do not add wear.
