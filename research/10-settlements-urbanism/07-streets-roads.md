Deep research request: Streets and roads: design and evolution

Context: I'm building The Civilization Engine (TCE), an endless, realistic agent-based simulation. Autonomous people found settlements, grow them into cities, and develop their own governments, laws, economies, technologies and architecture from authored building blocks. Research question: How were streets and roads designed, surfaced, maintained and upgraded?

- Road hierarchies and triggers for upgrading
- Maintenance organization and costs
- Intersections and plazas

3. Variation: how this differed across eras (foragers, early farming, pre-industrial, industrial, modern) and across world regions, not only Europe.
4. Stylized facts: real-world patterns a correct simulation should reproduce, with numbers where possible.
5. Modeling recommendation: how to represent this with individual agents and institutions, what to simplify, and existing models or games that already do it.
